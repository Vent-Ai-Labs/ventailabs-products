Questions

Straight answers

Will callers know it's not a person?

Yes, if you want. We set the greeting to say it's an automated assistant for your business. If a caller asks for a person, it connects them or books a callback.

What if it can't handle a call?

It hands off. Urgent calls go to your on-call number. Everything else gets a callback booked. No caller is left stuck.

Will it give out wrong prices?

No. It only says prices you approve. Otherwise it takes the details and someone on your team follows up.

What does phone usage cost?

It depends on how many calls you get. The voice and text companies bill you directly, at cost. We'll estimate it with you on the call.

Do I need approval to send texts?

Yes. US carriers require business texting to be registered (it's called A2P 10DLC). We walk you through it. Every text includes opt-out wording.

Who owns it?

You do. The accounts are in your name. You keep the script, the workflows and the call data, even if you cancel.
