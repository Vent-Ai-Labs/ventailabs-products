What you get

A complete GoHighLevel setup, built in your account.

  • Instant reply to every new leadA text and an email within seconds, plus an alert to the right person. Leads are shared out in turn.
  • A 7-stage pipelineNew, Contacted, Qualified, Estimate Sent, Booked, Won, Review. Each move starts the next step.
  • Missed-call text backMissed callers get a text and show up as a new lead.
