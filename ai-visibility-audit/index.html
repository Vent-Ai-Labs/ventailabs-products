Questions

No. No one can. We fix what these tools look at, and we show you the before and after.

Is campwarner.com a client?

No. It's a sample run on a public website, to show you the report. Its score only counts what we could check from the web.

Why are some checks “not checked” in the sample?

Some checks need a live ChatGPT, Gemini or Google session. In a paid audit we run those. In the sample we didn't, so we don't guess a score.
